Day 1 · With Christin
Coached Session
Lower Body · Glutes · Core
▼
Your coached day. Christin leads everything. Focus on form, feel every rep, trust the cues.
KAS Glute Bridge (barbell)
4 × 10–12
Small pelvic tilt at the top. Squeeze and hold. Isolates the glute max differently from a standard hip thrust.
Elevated RDL (deficit plates)
4 × 8–10
Standing on plates increases your range of motion. Deeper hamstring stretch. Push hips back, feel the stretch, drive through.
KB Dead Stop Split Squat
3 × 8 each
KB touches floor at the bottom — dead pause — then drive up from nothing. No momentum. Front glute does all the work.
Single Leg RDL + Cable Kickback
3 × each
Single leg RDL: hold weight in opposite hand, hinge forward, feel hamstring stretch. Kickback: ankle strap, kick back and hold 1 second.
Pallof Press + Copenhagen Plank
3 × each
Core finisher. Pallof: press cable out and hold 2 seconds. Copenhagen: side plank with top foot on bench. Hip stability and inner thigh.
Day 2 · Solo
Upper Body Pull
Back · Rear Delts · Biceps
▼
Back definition day. Every exercise here creates the strong defined look across your back and shoulders.
Wide Grip Lat Pulldown
4 × 10–12
Wide overhand grip. Creates the V-shape width in your back. Pull to collarbone, lean back slightly, squeeze hard.
Single Arm Cable Row
4 × 10–12 each
Low pulley. Each side independently — no dominant side cheating. Drive elbow back and up, hold 1 second at top.
Cable Rear Delt Fly + Face Pull
3 × each
Rear delt fly: both arms wide arc. Face pull: elbows high and wide, hold 1 second. Joint health — never skip this.
EZ Bar Curl
3 × 10–12
Full range of motion. Control the lowering over 2 counts — that is where the muscle builds.
🚣 Finisher Circuit · 3 rounds
Row 1 min → KB Swings 45s → Med Ball Slam 45s. Rest 90s between rounds. Effort 7/10.
Day 3 · Solo
Lower Body — Quads
Quads · Inner Thigh · No glute overlap
▼
QUAD day — not glute day. You can do this back to back with your coached session because the muscles are completely different.
Landmine Goblet Squat (heels elevated)
4 × 10–12
Heels on plates, hold end of landmine bar at chest. Squat straight down keeping chest tall. Pure quad focus.
Leg Press (mid foot, quad focus)
4 × 10–12
Mid plate — NOT high and wide. That is glutes. Mid plate is quad focus. Control down slowly.
Walking Lunge + Leg Extension
3 × each
Lunges: stay upright for quad focus. Extension: extend fully, hold 1 second, lower over 3 counts.
Barbell Spanish Squat
3 × 10–12
Bar at shin height on safety pins. Press calves into bar, squat with completely upright torso. Pure quad — your quads do everything.
Lateral Lunge + Cable Adduction
3 × each
Inner thigh and quad together. Cable adduction with ankle strap — hold 1 second at full contraction.
🚶 Cardio Finish
15–20 min incline treadmill walk. Quads done, glutes fresh. Fat burning without taxing recovery.
Day 4 · Solo
Upper Body Push
Chest · Shoulders · Triceps
▼
Strong defined arms come from training them twice a week. RPE 7–8 on all working sets.
Incline DB Press + Cable Fly (superset)
3 × each
Incline: lower slowly over 2 counts. Cable fly: wide arc, feel the stretch across chest. Two angles on the same muscle.
Arnold Press + Cable Lateral Raise (superset)
3 × each
Arnold: rotate palms as you press. Lateral raise: raise to shoulder height, hold 1 second, do not shrug.
Tricep Dip + Overhead Extension (superset)
3 × each
Dips hit all three heads. Overhead extension stretches the long head. Most complete tricep development available.
Dead Bug + Pallof Press (core finisher)
3 × each
Dead bug: lower back into floor. Pallof: press cable out and hold 2 seconds. Core stability — not crunches.
🚣 Cardio Finish
10 min rowing machine. Chest done — rowing is mostly back and legs. Clean finish.

During Your Period
What to do differently
🍽️ Eat more
Increase calories by 150–250 on days 1–3. Your body needs more energy. Add an extra snack. Do not restrict.
⚖️ Ignore the scale
Water retention during your cycle adds 1–5 lbs. This is hormonal — not fat. The scale is lying.
🏋️ Adjust training
Heavy days 1–2: reduce load 20–30%, keep the movement. Mobility day is always a yes.
💊 Hashimoto's note
Hormonal fluctuations can trigger flares. If fatigue is severe — mobility only, extra protein, extra rest. This is smart, not failure.
