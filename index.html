Current Phase
Return to Building · Back Focus
6 weeks off means we rebuild the neural connection before we push load. You have two back sessions per week now — one with Christin on Thursdays, one solo on Mondays. Back definition requires frequency. This is how we get there.
Thursday · With Christin
Coached Session
Upper Body · Back · Rows · Rear Delts
▼
Your coached day. Christin teaches back patterns the same way she taught you the hip thrust. Feel every rep, take the cues, trust the process. This is where your back definition is built.
Barbell Bent Over Row
4 × 8–10
Hinge at hips, back flat, bar to lower chest. Drive elbows back and up. Squeeze shoulder blades together at the top. Foundation of back thickness.
Single Arm Dumbbell Row
3 × 10–12 each
Bench supported. Drive elbow to ceiling — not your hand. Shoulder blade into your back pocket. Hold 1 second at top.
Wide Grip Lat Pulldown
4 × 10–12
Creates V-shape width in your back. Pull to your collarbone, lean back slightly. Feel it in the sides of your back — not your arms.
Seated Cable Row (close grip)
3 × 12–15
Mid-back thickness. Drive elbows back, hold 1 second at full contraction. Squeeze shoulder blades together.
Straight Arm Pulldown
3 × 12–15
Arms stay straight, pull cable from overhead to thighs. Directly isolates the lower lat — visible back definition comes from here.
Cable Face Pull
3 × 15–20
Rear delts and rotator cuff. Pull rope to face, elbows high and wide, hold 1 second. Joint health — never skipped.
Dead Bug + Pallof Press
3 × each
Core finisher. Dead bug: lower back into floor, exhale as you extend. Pallof: press cable out and hold 2 seconds.
Monday · Solo
Upper Body Pull
Back · Rear Delts · Biceps · Definition
▼
Second back day — different exercises from Thursday so nothing repeats. Your back definition day on your own. You know these movements. Trust what you have built.
Reverse Grip Lat Pulldown
4 × 8–12
Underhand shoulder-width grip. Shifts load to lower lat and biceps. Different feel from wide grip on Thursday. Pull to upper chest, squeeze hard.
Chest Supported DB Row
4 × 10–12
Lie face down on incline bench. Chest fully supported — lower back cannot help at all. Drive elbows back and up, hold 1 second. Honest back work.
Cable Rear Delt Fly (bilateral)
3 × 12–15
Both arms simultaneously. Cables at shoulder height, wide arc, hold 1 second at the back. Rear delt definition.
Cable Face Pull
3 × 15–20
Joint health — in every session. Elbows high and wide, hold 1 second. Never skip this.
EZ Bar Curl
3 × 10–12
Full range, both arms together. Control the lowering over 2 counts. Builds peak bicep strength.
🚣 Finisher · 3 rounds
Row 1 min → KB Swings 45s → Med Ball Slam 45s. Rest 90s between rounds. Effort 7/10.
Tuesday · Solo
Lower Body — Glutes
Glutes · Hamstrings · Hip Stability
▼
Glute day on your own. Different movements from your coached session so nothing overlaps. Feel your glutes on every single rep.
Glute Bridge (barbell)
4 × 12–15
Flat on floor. Drive through heels, squeeze hard at top, hold 2 seconds. Different mechanics from hip thrust. Add load as weeks progress.
Romanian Deadlift (dumbbells)
3 × 10–12
Push hips back — not down. Feel the hamstring stretch at the bottom. Drive through heels to stand, squeeze glutes at top. Back flat throughout.
Cable Kickback (ankle strap)
3 × 15 each
Face the cable, hinge slightly, kick straight back squeezing glute hard at top. Hold 1 second. Feel glute — not lower back.
Single Leg Hamstring Curl
3 × 10–12 each
Each hamstring works independently. Control the lowering slowly over 3 counts — the eccentric builds the muscle.
Step Up (box or bench)
3 × 10 each
Drive through the heel of the elevated foot. Do not push off the floor foot. Control the step down slowly.
🦵 Hip Stability Finisher
Clamshell 15–20 each side · Hip flexor march 10 each leg. Non-negotiable. Directly addresses hip popping.
Saturday · Solo
Lower Body — Quads
Quads · Inner Thigh · No glute overlap
▼
Quad day — not glute day. Can be done back to back with your glute session because the muscles are completely different. Feel your quads doing the work throughout.
Landmine Goblet Squat (heels elevated)
4 × 10–12
Heels on plates, hold end of landmine bar at chest. Squat straight down keeping chest tall. Pure quad focus.
Leg Press (mid foot, quad focus)
4 × 10–12
Mid plate — not high and wide. That is glutes. Mid plate is quad focus. Control down slowly over 2 counts.
Walking Lunge (dumbbells)
3 × 12 each
Keep torso upright for quad focus. Forward lean shifts load to glutes. Step, lower, drive through front heel, step forward.
Barbell Spanish Squat
3 × 10–12
Bar at shin height on safety pins. Press calves into bar, squat with completely upright torso. Your quads do everything.
Leg Extension + Cable Adduction
3 × each
Extension: extend fully, hold 1 second, lower over 3 counts. Adduction: ankle strap, pull leg across body, hold 1 second. Inner thigh isolation.
🚶 Cardio Finish
15–20 min incline treadmill walk. Quads done, glutes fresh. Fat burning without taxing recovery.
Sunday · Solo
Upper Body Push
Chest · Shoulders · Triceps · Strong Arms
▼
Push day — chest, shoulders, and triceps. Strong defined arms come from training them consistently. Everything here is different from your back days.
Incline DB Press + Cable Fly (superset)
3 × each
Incline: lower slowly over 2 counts, feel it across chest. Cable fly immediately after: wide arc, feel the stretch. Two chest angles back to back.
Arnold Press + Cable Lateral Raise (superset)
3 × each
Arnold: rotate palms as you press overhead. Cable lateral: raise to shoulder height, hold 1 second, do not shrug.
Tricep Dip + Overhead Extension (superset)
3 × each
Dips hit all three heads. Overhead extension stretches the long head. Most complete tricep stimulus available.
Weighted Dead Bug + Pallof Press
3 × each
Core finisher. Dead bug: lower back into floor. Pallof: press out and hold 2 seconds. Core stability is training.
🚣 Cardio Finish
10 min rowing machine. Chest done — rowing is mostly back and legs. Clean finish.
